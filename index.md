# Studio Light One — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

Studio Light One is a Windows PDF application published by Venkata Koteswara Rao ravipati. This policy explains how the application handles information and how to contact its publisher. Privacy contact: Venkatravipati46@gmail.com.

## Information used for your account

When you create an account, Studio Light One sends the name, email address, and password you provide to Google Firebase Authentication to create and manage your account. Firebase Authentication processes sign-in credentials, account identifiers, authentication tokens, and email verification and password reset requests. Passwords are sent over HTTPS; Studio Light One does not store your password in its local settings.

Google may process technical information, including IP addresses and user-agent information, to secure authentication and prevent abuse. For information about Google's handling of Firebase data, see https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## Information stored on your device

Studio Light One stores preferences, such as appearance and annotation settings, and a list of recently opened file paths on your device. If you choose to remember your sign-in, it stores an authentication refresh token locally using Windows data protection. The remembered sign-in expires after the application has been closed for more than one hour. Signing out removes the remembered session from the application's local settings.

## Your PDF files

Studio Light One opens and edits PDF files you select on your computer. PDF contents and annotations are processed locally; the application does not upload your PDF files to Firebase as part of ordinary viewing or editing. Files you explicitly save or export are written to the location you select.

## How information is used

Studio Light One uses account information to create accounts, authenticate users, verify email addresses, restore sign-in sessions when requested, and send password reset messages. Locally stored preferences support the settings and file history you choose to use.

## Sharing and storage

Studio Light One uses Google Firebase Authentication as its authentication service provider. Authentication data is sent to Google's services as needed for the functions described above. The publisher does not sell your account information. PDF files opened locally are not transferred to Firebase by the application's ordinary PDF workflow.

## Retention and your choices

Account information remains in Firebase Authentication until it is deleted or retention is otherwise required. To request access to or deletion of your account, contact the publisher at venkatravipati46@gmail.com. The publisher will verify that the request relates to your account before acting on it. Signing out clears the application's remembered session; it does not delete your Firebase account. You can manage files stored on your device using Windows.

## Security

The application uses HTTPS for Firebase Authentication requests and Windows data protection for locally remembered sign-in tokens. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes

The publisher may update this policy when the application or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
